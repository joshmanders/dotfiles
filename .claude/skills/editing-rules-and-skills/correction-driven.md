# Correction-Driven Work

Encodes a behavioral lesson. Every home stays terse and calm, matching its neighbors; there's no emphatic voice. For which home, see `placement.md`.

## The incident

It happened elsewhere and lives in Josh's head, not your context. Ask him for it; never invent or reconstruct it.

Acceptable without a specific incident:
- A stated principle ("Josh maintains control over what goes out under his name")
- A repeated preference ("Josh has corrected this repeatedly")
- A concrete cost ("confirming costs one round trip; the wrong action can lose work or leak secrets")

Unacceptable: fabricated specifics ("During a session in March...") or vague implied ones ("There was an incident where...").

## Process

1. Get the incident from Josh.
2. Pick the home per `placement.md`.
3. Keep the lesson's meaning identical. To soften, tighten, or expand it, ask first.
4. When touching multiple files, present one file's diff and wait for validation before the rest.
